# TechStore 6.3 — partial returns and installment cancellation

## Scope
- Owner can return selected quantities from fully paid cash invoices in the new ledger, or cancel an entire installment invoice. All goods must be inspected and resalable; missing products/cost, excess quantities, closed cash day, insufficient recorded cash and prior incompatible returns are rejected. Multi-unit lines with one serial must be returned together. Installment partial returns and damaged-item stock classification are not supported.
- Original invoices are immutable. New adjustment lines record original price/cost and cumulative returned quantities, with request-ID idempotency. Cash refunds and cancellation of uncollected debt post separate balanced entries; returned stock is restored at original unit cost using weighted average and cost is reversed. New ledger net revenue/cost and customer balances update. Legacy reports retain original gross revenue and a margin adjustment; not full consolidated accounting.
- Returned warranty quantities are reduced; fully returned lines are cancelled. Cancelled installment schedules show no further due amounts. Receipts after full cancellation are rejected. Original and current-balance invoice printing and adjustment notes include status; actual printer output untested.
- New payroll commission calculations subtract later returns known at the time of statement issuance. Previously issued payroll statements remain unchanged.
- Database access: active session and admin-only writes, authorized invoice reads, costs excluded from customer and cashier adjustment history, no direct table access. Original full refund workflow blocked if new adjustments exist.
- SQL rollback tests passed partial quantity refund, warranty quantity, idempotency, excess return denial, legacy full-refund guard, installment cash/debt split, cancellation schedule, payment denial, stock restoration, net summary and customer cost privacy. No real invoices/stock changed. JS syntax, print markup and path/cache checks passed. Actual browser, payment handling and physical printer E2E not performed.
- The 6.2 reorganization and its limitations remain. Supplier subledger and branch reports are separate, staff controls cover named workflows rather than all legacy routes, public gallery objects are public, older embedded photos are not migrated, app manifest identity is unchanged. Security advisories on extension location and leaked password protection remain; no independent penetration test or production certification.
